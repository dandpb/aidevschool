## Hook
You need a supplier's phone number. Do you search the web, ask an AI to invent a
draft, or both — and why does the choice matter?

## Worked example
Search tools look up pages and records that already exist. They point you to sources.
An AI writing assistant produces new wording from patterns it learned. Great for a
first draft or a summary shape. Weak as your only source for a hard fact. Problem
solving with AI often means breaking a task into steps, trying options, and checking
results. Search finds; generation drafts; you still verify what matters.

## Practice
Level 1 — choose search, AI draft, or both for each need:
(1) official opening hours for a city office; (2) three subject lines to test;
(3) a court filing deadline from a public site.
(Hint: search, AI draft, search.)

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Yes — search finds existing sources; AI drafts from patterns. Real problem
  solving still needs your checks on what must be true."
- On fail: "Split the jobs: look up hard facts; use AI to draft or reshape; verify
  anything you will rely on."
