## Hook
Someone pastes a full staff file into a public chatbot "just to polish the tone."
What leaves your control the moment they hit send?

## Worked example
Anything you type can be stored, reviewed, or reused outside your team. Names,
birth dates, emails, phones, ID numbers, and home addresses are personal data.
They belong in your trusted systems, not in a chatbot box. Strip every personal
item first. Keep the request about tone or structure only. The AI does not need
the real details to draft wording.

## Practice
Level 2 — no list of hints. Spot every personal item in this line and rewrite it
safely: "Call Diego Alvarez, DOB 3 July 1990, ID BR-44199203, at +55 21 98877-1200
or diego.alvarez@mailtest.org; home Rua das Flores 88, Rio."

## Gate task
Final task. A colleague wants chatbot help on the email below. Send back the same
email with every piece of personal data replaced by [REDACTED] — exactly that
token, in square brackets.

Subject: Client follow-up draft needed

Hi team — please polish a reminder for Priya Nair, born 22 August 1979. Reach her
at priya.nair@demo-inbox.com or +44 7700 900128. Her staff ID is PN-19790822-44.
Home address on file: 17 Baker Lane, Flat 3, Leeds LS1 4DY.

## Reply format
Send back the same email with each personal item replaced by [REDACTED].

## Feedback snippets

- On fail: "Hunt for name, birth date, email, phone, ID, and home address. Each one
