## Hook
If chatbots vanished tomorrow, would AI leave your day — or stay in quieter places?

## Worked example
Maps, spam filters, recommendations, and photo grouping already use learned patterns.
They rank and sort without a long conversation. Chat is one interface. Pattern-based
sorting in the background is how most people meet AI every day.

## Practice
Level 2 — name three places AI likely touches a normal weekday for you. One short
line each. No need for brand names.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Right — daily AI is often quiet sorting and suggesting, not only chat.
  Notice it, then decide if the suggestion fits."
- On fail: "Look for ranking, suggesting, and pattern matching in apps you already use.
  Fixed timers and plain calculators are different from that."
