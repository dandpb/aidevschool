## Hook
Your manager loves an AI rewrite of a partner's sealed deck. They want it on the
company blog today. What must be true before you hit publish?

## Worked example
AI does not wash away rights. If the source was owned, restricted, or personal,
you still need permission and a lawful path. Copyright can cover wording and
images even when you only "inspired" a new draft. Consent is specific: allowed
for one use is not allowed for every use. You stay the person who must check.

## Practice
Level 2 — decide alone. You have a customer's support transcript and a free
public chatbot. In two sentences, say whether you may paste it, and what you
would do instead if not.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Right — rights and consent travel with the work. AI speed never
  replaces permission."
- On fail: "Slow down at the source. If you do not own it and nobody cleared it,
  do not feed it or publish it."
