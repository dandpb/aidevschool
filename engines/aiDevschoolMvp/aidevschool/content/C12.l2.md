## Hook
A teammate pastes "Do the report thing" into a chatbot and gets junk back. What did
the instructions leave out?

## Worked example
The instructions you type for the AI (the prompt) are the whole brief it gets. Vague
input yields vague output. Strong instructions name three things. Task: the job to do.
Context: who it is for and the facts it needs. Format: length, tone, or structure.
Without those, the system fills gaps with generic pattern-matching. With them, you get
something you can use.

## Practice
Level 2 — on your own. Rewrite "Help with my presentation" into Task, Context, and
Format. Use three labeled lines.

## Gate task
Final task — no help this time. Rewrite this prompt so it has all three parts:
'Tell me about marketing.'

## Reply format
Reply with exactly three lines, in this order: Task: / Context: / Format: — one line each.

## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Clean brief — the job, the situation, and the shape are explicit. That is
  how you steer the answer."
- On fail: "Do not answer the vague ask. Rewrite it as Task, Context, and Format, one
  line each."
