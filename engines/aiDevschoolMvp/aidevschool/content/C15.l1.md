## Hook
Your team chat is full of bold claims about a product launch. Screenshots, numbers,
and "everyone agrees" fly by. Which ones would you trust enough to act on?

## Worked example
Sam drafts a short status note from chat messages and an AI summary. One line quotes
"47% growth" with no source. Another says a screenshot proves a rival is shutting
down. A third says the AI "confirmed" the same claim. A fourth says the comment thread
agrees, so it must be true. None of those are checks. A number needs a source. A
picture proves someone shared it, not that it is true. An AI restating a claim is the
same claim again. Group agreement is opinion. Real verification means leaving the chat
and checking an independent record.

## Practice
Level 1 — we flag one weak claim together. Reply with its number: (1) "Our store
opens at 9 a.m. on weekdays, per the posted hours."; (2) "Sales jumped 80% last week
— I saw it in a group chat."; (3) "The meeting room holds twelve chairs."

## Gate task
Check time. A short workplace update is below. Four of the eight claims are weak or
fabricated. Reply with the numbers of the false or unsupported claims, comma-separated.

1. The North River store opened a second checkout lane on Monday after a staff vote.
2. Foot traffic is up 63% this month according to a figure someone repeated in chat.
3. The manager posted the new Saturday hours on the staff board by the break room.
4. A screenshot of a rival's home page proves they are closing all city branches next week.
5. Two team leads agreed in writing to share one stockroom key during the remodel.
6. An AI assistant restated the rival-closure claim, so the rumor is now confirmed.
7. Most comments under the announcement agree the launch will beat last year's sales.
8. The inventory sheet from Tuesday still lists twelve spare card readers in storage.

## Reply format
Reply with the numbers of the false or unsupported claims, comma-separated, like: 2, 4, 6, 7.

## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Yes — numbers need sources, screenshots are not proof, AI repeats are not
  new evidence, and comment threads are opinion."
- On fail: "Mark claims that lean on an unsourced number, a screenshot as proof, an AI
  restating a rumor, or 'everyone in the thread agrees.'"
