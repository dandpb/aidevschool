## Hook
A friend says the AI "knows everything" and "thinks like a person." That sounds
powerful. What is AI actually doing when it answers you?

## Worked example
Sam asks an AI to finish a short work email. The reply sounds smooth and polite.
It is not a mind at work. The AI is software that learned patterns from many
examples during its practice phase. When you ask, it predicts what wording usually
comes next. That is useful. It is not the same as understanding, feeling, or
checking the real world. A calculator follows fixed math steps. An AI guesses from
patterns it has seen. Fluent wording is not proof of thought.
