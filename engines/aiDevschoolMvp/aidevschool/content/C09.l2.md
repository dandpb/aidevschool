## Hook
Your boss asks, "Can we tune this detector until mistakes disappear?" What trade-off do
you need ready before you answer?

## Worked example
Systems that sort cases into labels use patterns, so they produce false alarms and
missed cases. Push the dial to catch more real problems, and more fine cases get
flagged. Push the other way, and more real problems slip through. Cost decides the
balance — in fraud, health, hiring, or spam. Perfection is not on the menu. Review
still matters when the stakes are high.

## Practice
Level 2 — pick one workplace sorter (spam, fraud, or safety). In three sentences, name
both error types, what each costs there, and which way you would lean the balance.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.

Then, imagine a colleague asks you to explain it. Explain why a classifier makes mistakes to them in your own words, in 3 to 5 sentences.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets

- On fail: "Rebuild the story: two mistake types, they trade off, costs pick the balance,
