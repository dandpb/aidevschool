## Hook
When a task mixes facts and wording, what do you assign to search, to AI, and to
yourself?

## Worked example
Search retrieves what already exists. Generative AI produces new wording from learned
patterns. Good problem solving sequences those strengths: find sources, draft options,
compare, and verify the claims that matter. Skipping the check is how fluent errors
slip through.

## Practice
Level 2 — outline a three-step plan for "prepare a short supplier update with the
real delivery date." Say what you search, what you ask the AI, and what you check.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Yes — search finds existing sources; AI drafts from patterns. Real problem
  solving still needs your checks on what must be true."
- On fail: "Split the jobs: look up hard facts; use AI to draft or reshape; verify
  anything you will rely on."
