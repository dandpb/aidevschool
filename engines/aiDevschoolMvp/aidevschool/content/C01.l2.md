## Hook
People talk about AI as if it were a colleague with a brain. Where does that picture
help, and where does it mislead you?

## Worked example
An AI can draft a meeting note that reads natural. That skill comes from patterns
learned in its practice phase, not from lived experience. It predicts wording that
often fits. It does not care, intend, or verify the world the way a person does.
Treat it as a fast pattern tool. Do not treat it as a mind that "knows."

## Practice
Level 2 — without hints, label each as "rules," "learned," or "neither":
(1) a light switch; (2) a spam filter improved by many past emails; (3) a friend
recalling a holiday. Reply in three short labels.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Yes — AI is pattern-learning software, not a person and not a guarantee.
  Useful drafts still need your judgment."
- On fail: "Hold the core idea: AI predicts from examples it practiced on. Fluent is
  not the same as knowing, and fixed tools are not the same as learned patterns."
