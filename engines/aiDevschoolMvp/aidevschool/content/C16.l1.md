## Hook
A hiring tool ranks men higher than equally skilled women. Nobody typed that rule
in. Where did the unfair tilt come from?

## Worked example
A company trains a résumé screener on ten years of past hires. Most past hires
were men in one role. The system learns that pattern and ranks similar men
higher. The tilt did not start as a mean rule. It started in the examples used
during the AI's practice phase, when it learns from examples. Outputs echo the
gaps and habits in those examples. Biased data in means biased answers out. You
spot the risk by asking whose stories filled the examples.

## Practice
Level 1 — fill the blank together. A loan tool rejects more applicants from one
neighborhood. The likely cause is: the examples used in the AI's practice phase
______ that neighborhood. Reply with one short phrase.

## Feedback snippets
