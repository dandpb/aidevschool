## Hook
Someone says, "Just tell the AI the rules and it will know." What is missing from that
picture of how these systems actually improve?

## Worked example
AI improves by adjusting to patterns across many examples in a practice phase. Later it
reuses that know-how on new cases. It is closer to learning from experience than to
storing one fixed rule for every future event. If practice cases are narrow, new cases
outside that world will often fail. Broad, honest examples teach broader patterns.

## Practice
Level 2 — explain in two sentences how practicing on many labeled support tickets could
help an AI sort new tickets — and name one way that learning can still fail.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Well reasoned — pattern learning from examples explains both the power and
  the blind spots when the next case looks different."
- On fail: "Separate practice from use: practice finds patterns in examples; use applies
  them to something new, with no promise of perfection."
