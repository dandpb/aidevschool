## Hook
"Smart software" can mean a strict checklist or a system trained on examples. How do
you tell which kind you are using?

## Worked example
Fixed rules fire the same way every time: if this, then that. Learned systems adjust
from patterns in examples during the practice phase. Rules miss unlisted cases.
Learned tools can absorb bad patterns. Naming which kind you face helps you predict
the failure.

## Practice
Level 2 — write one workplace rule example and one learned-behavior example. One
sentence each. No hints beyond the labels.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Clear — rules are if-then people write; learned behavior comes from patterns
  in examples. Each fails differently."
- On fail: "Ask: did a person write a fixed if-then, or did the system pick up patterns
  from many examples? That split is the whole lesson."
