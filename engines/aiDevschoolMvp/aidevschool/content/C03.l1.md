## Hook
One system always refunds orders under $20. Another starts spotting fraud after seeing
thousands of past cases. Why do those two "smart" tools feel so different?

## Worked example
A door policy says: "If badge is valid, open." That is a rule people wrote. Clear in,
clear out. A photo tool learns "cat" after many labeled cat pictures in its practice
phase. Nobody listed every whisker rule by hand. Rules are instructions people set.
Learned behavior comes from patterns in examples. Both can be useful. They fail in
different ways. A rule misses cases nobody wrote down. A learned system can copy bad
patterns from its examples.
