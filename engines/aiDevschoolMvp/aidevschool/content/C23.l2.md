## Hook
If nobody can tell where AI stopped and you started, who can trust the result?

## Worked example
Disclose AI help in plain words when others will rely on the work. Good notes are
specific and short. Bad notes hide the help or dump blame on the tool. Trust
breaks faster from silence than from honesty. You remain accountable after any
disclosure.

## Practice
Level 2 — write one disclosure sentence you could put at the top of a real work
email. No template given. Keep it plain.

## Gate task
A colleague asks you to send the weekly summary you drafted with AI help, and your
manager will use it to decide the budget. What do you do?
A) Send it as-is and say nothing about the AI.
B) Send it with a short note that AI helped draft it and that you checked the figures.
C) Refuse to use AI at all for any work.

Then, imagine a colleague asks you to explain it. Explain why you should say when AI helped to them in your own words, in 3 to 5 sentences.
## Reply format
Reply with one letter (A, B, or C) and one line on why.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Clear and honest — disclosure builds trust, and accountability stays
  with you."
- On fail: "Name the AI help in plain words and keep ownership. Silence is not a
  safety plan."
