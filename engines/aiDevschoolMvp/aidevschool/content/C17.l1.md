## Hook
A colleague is about to paste a customer's full details into a chatbot to "draft a
polite reply." What could go wrong, and what should never go into that box?

## Worked example
Maya wants an AI to rewrite an email about a client, Marta. The email carries Marta's
full name, date of birth, personal email, phone, national ID, and home address. Once
that text leaves for a model, Maya no longer controls where it is stored or who sees it.
The safe habit: swap every piece of personal data for a fixed placeholder before the text
touches any AI, and keep real identifiers in your own trusted system. The AI can still
draft the tone and structure; it does not need the real data.

## Practice
Level 1 — name the personal items in this line, one per line: "Please email Joana Silva
at joana.silva@work.com or call +55 11 99876-5432 about her order."

## Gate task
Final task. A colleague wants to paste the email below into a chatbot to get a reply
drafted. Send back the same email, but replace every piece of personal data with
[REDACTED] — exactly that token, in square brackets.

Hi Ana — please draft a polite reminder for Marta Kowalska, born 14 March 1982. Her personal email is marta.kowalska@examplemail.com and her mobile is +48 601 223 445. Her national ID is 82031455667. She lives at ul. Słoneczna 12/4, 00-789 Warszawa.

## Reply format
Send back the same email with each personal item replaced by [REDACTED].

## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Exactly — names, dates, contact details, IDs, and addresses stay out. The AI
  drafts the tone; your real data stays under your control."
- On fail: "Every name, number, email, ID, and address is personal data. Replace each with
  [REDACTED] before the text reaches any AI."
