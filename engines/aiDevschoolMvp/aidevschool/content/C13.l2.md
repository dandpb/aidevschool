## Hook
The first AI draft missed the mark. Do you blame the tool, or do you tighten the brief
and try again?

## Worked example
Weak instructions waste a turn. Strong iteration keeps the same output goal and sharpens
the brief. You add missing context, lock a format, give a tiny example, or set a hard
limit. You send improved instructions you type for the AI (an improved prompt) — not the
finished content, and not a lecture about prompting. Each pass should be short enough to
paste straight into the chat.

## Practice
Level 2 — alone. Improve "Make this messy shift handover clearer for the next nurse"
into Task, Context, and Format. Add at least one concrete upgrade. Do not write the
handover text.

## Gate task
Final task — no help this time. Rewrite this prompt so it has all three parts:
'Give me some tips for the team offsite.'

Then, imagine a colleague asks you to explain it. Explain why the AI sounds so sure of itself to them in your own words, in 3 to 5 sentences.
## Reply format
Reply with exactly three lines, in this order: Task: / Context: / Format: — one line each.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "That is iteration — same job, sharper brief, paste-ready. Keep upgrading
  instead of settling."
- On fail: "The reply missed a part. Name the Task, the Context, and the Format, one
  clear line each, and the next attempt improves."
