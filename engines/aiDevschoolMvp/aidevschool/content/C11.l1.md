## Hook
An AI answers your question in a calm, certain voice. Later you learn a key fact was
wrong. If it sounded so sure, why was it still mistaken?

## Worked example
Large language systems write by guessing the next likely chunk of text. That skill
comes from the AI's practice phase, when it learns from examples of fluent writing.
Smooth, confident sentences are exactly what those examples rewarded. The system has
no human-style beliefs behind the words. It is not checking a mental notebook of truth.
It is continuing a pattern that sounds right. So confidence is a side effect of fluent
prediction, not proof that it "knows."
