## Hook
People swing between "AI takes every job" and "nothing changes." What steadier story
helps you act?

## Worked example
A job is a bundle of tasks. Some get faster with AI. New ones appear: checking,
guiding, and explaining. Roles usually shift rather than vanish overnight. Wider
effects — fairness, new kinds of work, energy use, and rules like the EU AI Act —
matter too. The useful response is critical skill, not fear or blind adoption.

## Practice
Level 2 — in two short sentences, tell a colleague why both "AI takes every job" and
"nothing changes" are weak guides for planning.

## Gate task
Your team is worried AI will change their jobs. What is the most balanced response
you can give?
A) "AI will replace everyone soon, so there is no point adapting."
B) "AI will change nothing; ignore it completely."
C) "Tasks will shift; let us learn to use AI well and keep human judgment in the loop."

Then, imagine a colleague asks you to explain it. Explain how AI is changing work and society to them in your own words, in 3 to 5 sentences.
## Reply format
Reply with one letter (A, B, or C) and one line on why.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Clear — task-level change, no false extremes, and a practical critical
  response."
- On fail: "Hold the middle: tasks shift, whole jobs rarely vanish overnight, and
  critical learning beats panic or denial."
