## Hook
Your manager asks why the company chatbot can be wrong while still sounding expert.
What short answer do you give?

## Worked example
A language AI predicts the next likely chunk of text from patterns learned in practice.
Those patterns reward smooth, certain-sounding sentences. Nothing in that process
requires a true belief or a fact check. So the system can invent a detail and still
sound authoritative. Confidence is a style of wording, not a signal that it "knows."
Treat important claims as drafts until you verify them.

## Practice
Level 2 — write two sentences to a non-technical friend. Explain why a confident AI
answer can still be false.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Exactly — fluency is the product; knowledge is not. Verify before you
  depend on a claim."
- On fail: "Ask what produces the tone: likely next words from practice, not a mind
  that knows."
