## Hook
A child sees many dogs and then points at a new puppy and says "dog." Nobody handed
over a full rulebook. How does that kind of learning map onto AI?

## Worked example
Think of learning to spot ripe mangoes at a market. After enough examples — color,
smell, soft spots — you notice patterns. You were not given one perfect formula. You
generalized from cases. During the AI's practice phase, when it learns from examples,
it adjusts internal dials so common patterns score higher. Later it applies that
know-how to new cases it has never seen. It is pattern learning, not a human-written
checklist for every situation. That is why examples matter so much, and why odd new
cases can still surprise it.
