## Hook
A teammate pastes a client's private report into a free AI tool, then posts the
rewrite online. Who owns what, and who had to say yes?

## Worked example
Three ideas keep you safe. Ownership: someone already holds rights in the text,
image, or data. Consent: you need permission before you reuse or share it.
Copyright: many creative works are protected, so copying them into an AI or out
of one can break the rules. A fluent rewrite does not erase those duties. If you
did not create it and nobody cleared it, treat it as not yours to feed or publish.

## Practice
Level 1 — choose together. Maya wants an AI to turn a vendor's paid white paper
into a public blog. Safest first step? (1) Paste it now; (2) ask the owner and
check the license; (3) change three words and post. Reply with 1, 2, or 3.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Clear — ownership, consent, and copyright still apply when AI helps.
  Permission and rights come before paste or publish."
- On fail: "Ask three questions: who owns this, who said yes, and what may I copy?
  A smooth AI rewrite does not answer them for you."
