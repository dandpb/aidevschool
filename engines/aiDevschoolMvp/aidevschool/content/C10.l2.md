## Hook
Someone claims chatbots "just search the web and paste." You suspect that is wrong.
What is actually going on when new text appears?

## Worked example
A generative system is a large web of tiny linked units (a neural network). In the
practice phase it saw huge piles of examples. Its internal dials were tuned to common
patterns. When you ask for something new, it does not fetch one saved answer. It
predicts the next small chunk of text or image that fits those patterns. Fluent output
is the goal of that design. Creation here means recombination of patterns, not memory
of one true page.

## Practice
Level 2 — on your own. In two short sentences, explain how generative AI can write a
new email subject line it has never stored word for word.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Sharp — new output is predicted piece by piece from tuned patterns, not
  pulled from a shelf of finished answers."
- On fail: "Separate storage from generation. The system predicts the next fitting
  chunk after practice on many examples."
