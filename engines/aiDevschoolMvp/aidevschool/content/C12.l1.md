## Hook
You text a new colleague: "Can you send me the thing for tomorrow's meeting?" What
could go wrong?

## Worked example
An AI is in the same spot — it only sees the instructions you type for it (your
prompt). Look: you type "Write something about our sales." It answers with three vague
paragraphs. Nothing you can use. Now give three parts. Task: "Write a three-sentence
update." Context: "Sales rose 10% in March." Format: "plain sentences, no jargon." The
answer is exactly what you needed. A good set of instructions = task + context +
format.

## Practice
Level 1 — fill the two gaps and reply with the full three lines:
Task: write a birthday message for Ana.
Context: she loves [gap 1].
Format: [gap 2].

## Gate task
Final task — no help this time. Rewrite this prompt so it has all three parts:
'Tell me about marketing.'

## Reply format
Reply with exactly three lines, in this order: Task: / Context: / Format: — one line each.

## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Passed — task, context, and format are all there. Tell the AI the job, the
  situation, and the shape of the answer."
- On fail: "Name the job first, then who it is for and the facts, then the shape of the
  answer."
