## Hook
A chatbot writes a poem in seconds. A photo app invents a new beach scene. How can a
machine make things that did not exist before?

## Worked example
Think of a huge web of tiny switches. Each switch passes a weak signal to its
neighbors. During the AI's practice phase, when it learns from examples, those
internal dials get tuned so common patterns light up more strongly. Later, when you
ask for a new sentence or image, the web predicts the next small piece that fits the
pattern. That web of tiny linked units is often called a neural network. Generative AI
builds new text or pictures from patterns, not from a fixed rulebook. It is not looking
up a stored answer. It is stitching likely pieces together.
