## Hook
You unlock your phone and a feed already feels "for you." Maps reroute before you ask.
Where is AI already sitting in an ordinary day?

## Worked example
Lina's morning is full of quiet AI. Her maps app predicts traffic from past trips.
Her email flags likely spam. A music app queues songs like ones she played. A photo
app groups faces it has seen before. None of these need a chat box. They learn
patterns from many examples, then rank or sort what you see next. Chat assistants are
only the visible tip. Much of daily AI is background sorting and suggesting.

## Feedback snippets

  Notice it, then decide if the suggestion fits."
- On fail: "Look for ranking, suggesting, and pattern matching in apps you already use.
