## Hook
Two teams use the same AI. One group keeps getting weaker results. How can the
same tool treat people so differently?

## Worked example
Past decisions become tomorrow's examples. If old choices favored one group, the
AI's practice phase locks that habit in. The system then produces answers that
look objective while repeating the tilt. Fairness is not a tone setting. It is a
check on who was in the examples and who the outputs help or harm.

## Practice
Level 2 — on your own. Name one workplace AI use and one uneven pattern you would
test for before trusting it. One or two sentences.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Strong — you traced the tilt back to examples and checks, not magic.
  That is how responsible teams catch bias early."
- On fail: "Look past confident wording. Ask which groups filled the examples and
  who the outputs help or harm."
