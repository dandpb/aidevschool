## Hook
Workplace AI is useful only inside a safe loop. What belongs in that loop, and what
never does?

## Worked example
Priya at Harborline Logistics drafts a delay update. She removes personal details,
gives the AI a clean outline, checks the draft against her notes, and gets approval.
Safe workflow means clean inputs, a clear task, human review, and a named owner.

## Practice
Level 2 — a client email needs a draft. List the three steps you will take, in order,
in your own words. Keep each step to one short line.

## Gate task
You work at Blue Orchard Bank. Your manager wants a short FAQ page about a new savings
product for the public website. You have a one-page fact sheet with approved wording.
A junior colleague wants real customer complaint emails in the chatbot for "tone."
Then they want the AI text posted live with no review.

What should you do?

A) Paste the real complaint emails so the tone feels authentic, then publish.
B) Generate the FAQ with AI from anything handy and skip review to meet the deadline.
C) Use only the approved fact sheet with no personal details. Edit the draft. Get
manager approval before publishing.

Reply with the letter of your choice and one line on why.

## Reply format
Reply with the letter of your choice and one line on why.

## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Right — approved facts in, personal data out, human edit, then approval."
- On fail: "Real customer mail never goes into the box. Review and approval are not
  optional extras."
