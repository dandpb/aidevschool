## Hook
You type a question and an answer appears in seconds. Did the AI just study the whole
internet again, or is something else going on behind that quick reply?

## Worked example
Compare two moments. First, a long practice phase: the AI sees huge piles of examples
and slowly tunes its internal dials. That step is heavy, rare, and usually done by the
builder — not by you in the chat. Second, everyday use: you ask, and the AI produces an
answer with the know-how it already has. That second step is lighter and fast. Baking
the cake is not the same as serving a slice. When you chat, you are almost always in
the serving step. You are not running the whole practice phase again with each message.

## Practice
Level 1 — label each moment **practice** or **use**: (1) engineers run the AI through
millions of labeled images for days; (2) you upload one photo and ask what animal it is.

## Gate task
The tutor will send you three short questions, one at a time or together. Answer each
with A, B, or C.
## Reply format
Reply with just the three letters in order, like: A C B.
## Feedback snippets
- On pass: "Clear split — practice builds the know-how; chat mostly uses it. You are
  usually eating the slice, not rebaking the cake."
- On fail: "Keep two timelines: the long practice phase that tunes dials, and the quick
  moment when the AI produces an answer for you."
