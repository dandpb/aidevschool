# AiDevSchool Founding Engineer — Publication Decision Memo

Status: Founder decision requested  
Owner: CEO  
Prepared: 2026-08-04

## Recommendation

Publish this as a full-time, remote US Founding Product Engineer role with a base salary of **USD $180,000–$210,000** and an initial option grant targeting **0.75%–1.25% fully diluted ownership**, subject to board approval, the equity plan, a standard four-year vesting schedule with a one-year cliff, and the candidate's experience. Require four hours of weekday overlap with **12:00–20:00 UTC** and describe occasional travel only if the company has a real, funded expectation for it.

This recommendation deliberately sits around the competitive middle of current first-engineer data rather than the top of the market. Pave's February 2025 Tier-1 US benchmark reports $187,000–$250,000 cash and roughly 0.33%–1.32% equity for founding engineers, with its published 50th, 75th, and 90th-percentile examples at $187,000/0.33%, $215,000/0.62%, and $235,000/1.24%. Carta's May 2026 report says median initial grants for AI/ML engineers rose 31% from January 2024 to February 2026 and that the smallest startups saw the largest increases. The proposed package therefore uses meaningful ownership to compete without pretending AiDevSchool should automatically pay a top-decile Tier-1 cash package.

## Compensation choices

### Option A — Balanced ownership (recommended)

- Base: USD $180,000–$210,000.
- Initial equity target: 0.75%–1.25% fully diluted, expressed in the offer as both share count and estimated percentage.
- Intended for: a true first product-engineering hire with broad 0-to-1 ownership.
- Tradeoff: credible cash plus meaningful founding-stage upside; requires sufficient runway and option-pool capacity.

### Option B — Cash-forward

- Base: USD $210,000–$235,000.
- Initial equity target: 0.40%–0.75% fully diluted.
- Intended for: competing more directly with senior Tier-1-market offers.
- Tradeoff: easier for candidates with higher fixed-cost needs, but consumes more runway and may feel less like a founding package.

### Option C — Equity-forward

- Base: USD $150,000–$180,000.
- Initial equity target: 1.25%–2.00% fully diluted.
- Intended for: candidates who explicitly prefer ownership and when the company must conserve cash.
- Tradeoff: materially narrows the candidate pool and should only be used when the candidate freely elects it from a cash-viable alternative.

Do not publish an open-ended or implausibly wide range. The final range should be the good-faith amount AiDevSchool is prepared to pay. New York guidance, for example, requires a genuine minimum and maximum for covered postings and treats remote roles as covered in some reporting relationships.

## Remote-work choices

### Option 1 — Remote US (recommended for the first hire)

- Eligible location: US states where AiDevSchool has confirmed it can employ or contract compliantly.
- Collaboration: four weekday hours overlapping 12:00–20:00 UTC.
- Travel: none unless a specific, funded expectation is approved.
- Benefit: one compensation currency and a narrower employment, tax, and pay-transparency review.

### Option 2 — Remote Americas

- Eligible location: named countries in the Americas supported by an approved employer-of-record or contractor model.
- Compensation: publish country or geographic bands, not a single US band represented as universal.
- Benefit: larger talent pool; cost and classification complexity increase.

### Option 3 — Global remote

- Eligible location: only named supported countries, despite the “global” sourcing intent.
- Compensation: define a transparent location policy and local bands before publishing.
- Benefit: broadest pool; highest compliance, payroll, timezone, benefits, and equity complexity. Not recommended as the first-hire default without operating infrastructure.

## Application-route recommendation

Use a short, privacy-reviewed application form rather than a public GitHub issue or a personal inbox. The form should collect only:

- name and contact email;
- location and work authorization/engagement eligibility;
- short mission-interest response;
- one shipped-product example and the candidate's personal contribution;
- optional portfolio, GitHub, or LinkedIn URL;
- accommodation request route; and
- consent/privacy notice with retention period.

Assign one monitored owner and a backup, send an automatic receipt, test submission and export end to end, restrict access, and define deletion timing before posting. Until that route exists, the role should not be published publicly.
