# Founding Product Engineer — Learning Platform

Status: Final terms approved; publication pending supported-jurisdiction and application-route setup  
Reports to: CEO/founder  
Company: AiDevSchool

## About AiDevSchool

AiDevSchool democratizes AI knowledge and application through short, practical lessons with a Duolingo-like rhythm. We serve both people who want to use AI without programming and developers who want to build robust software with AI.

Our open-source ecosystem already includes shared curricula, learner-state infrastructure, a no-code AI literacy experience, developer learning surfaces, and teaching games. We are hiring a founding product engineer to turn that strong foundation into one coherent, trustworthy learner journey.

## The role

You will own AiDevSchool's first reliable public learner path, from selecting a lesson through attempting it, receiving feedback, retrying, submitting evidence, and seeing independently verified progress.

This is a hands-on founding role across product and engineering. You will ship narrow vertical slices, work directly with learners, simplify a heterogeneous codebase, and establish the deployment and observability practices needed for a dependable product. You will also protect a central learning-integrity rule: the system or agent that produces work cannot verify its own evidence or declare mastery.

## What you will do

- Ship one focused, browser-accessible learning loop before expanding breadth.
- Build across TypeScript, React, Vite, and Python while respecting engine-local boundaries and shared curriculum and learner-state contracts.
- Integrate lessons, attempts, feedback, hints and retries, evidence capture, review scheduling, and independently verified progress.
- Make the no-code AI Literacy journey excellent first while keeping shared contracts useful to the developer track.
- Establish practical CI, preview deployment, monitoring, privacy-safe analytics, accessibility checks, and rollback.
- Consolidate or clearly bound overlapping product surfaces when evidence supports it.
- Interview learners, instrument the journey, and turn findings into small, testable product increments.
- Document durable decisions and make the repository easier for future hires and contributors.

## What we are looking for

You should apply if several of these describe you:

- You have personally shipped a web product from an ambiguous brief to real users.
- You are strong in TypeScript and React and comfortable working in Python.
- You can deliver a thin vertical slice across UI, state, APIs, tests, and deployment.
- You treat evaluation and evidence as software contracts, not merely prompt-writing concerns.
- You make principled simplification decisions in an existing, heterogeneous codebase.
- You communicate clearly with users and balance speed with educational integrity.

Experience in edtech, developer tools, LLM evaluation, accessible interaction design, learning analytics, local-first systems, or an early-stage startup is useful but not required. We value demonstrated judgment and shipped work over a particular degree, pedigree, or keyword match.

## How we work

- One learner and one shared curriculum can be served by multiple product engines.
- Learning progress is file-based and auditable where practical.
- A learner attempt plus independently verified evidence is required before mastery.
- We prefer focused product increments and measured outcomes to broad rewrites.
- We communicate in plain language, surface tradeoffs early, and distinguish shipped behavior from proposals.

## First 30 days

In your first month, you will run the primary product surfaces, map the learner loop and data boundaries, recommend one production surface, and deliver a preview-deployed vertical slice. Success means the independent verification boundary is tested, learner events are privacy-safe and observable, release operations are documented, and findings from at least five representative learner sessions shape the next roadmap. It does not require migrating every engine.

## Hiring process

1. A 30-minute conversation with the CEO covering motivation, product ownership, constraints, compensation, and availability.
2. A 60-minute discussion of a system you shipped, including tradeoffs, failures, instrumentation, and your contribution.
3. A paid, two-to-four-hour AiDevSchool repository work session. We will not use speculative production work without compensation and agreement.
4. A 45-minute learning-integrity review covering independent verification, accessibility, privacy, and attempt-before-solution behavior.
5. References and a mutual close.

Candidates are evaluated with the same written scorecard across product shipping and learner empathy, full-stack depth, systems judgment, evidence integrity, and founding-team communication. Reasonable interview accommodations are available.
