⚡ Bolt: optimize renderer sync loops to reduce gc pressure

💡 What: Removed `for...of` loops and `new Set()` allocations from the 60-FPS `sync()` methods in `BuildingsRenderer`, `ZonesRenderer`, and `RoadsRenderer`. The set allocation is now guarded by a size check (`map.size > source.length`).

📊 Impact: Completely eliminates allocations in the steady-state rendering path, reducing GC pauses and improving framerate stability.
