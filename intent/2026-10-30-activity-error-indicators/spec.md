# Specification

For an incorrect criterion or classification in a failed attempt, identify the selected incorrect control programmatically and give its item a visible, non-color-only cue. Preserve marks only for the submitted result, clear them during edits, keep correct choices unmarked and preserve scoring, hints and feedback. Verify real lessons in normal and forced colors, keyboard, edits and independent review.

Existing scores at or above the pass threshold may include incorrect checks; this change does not mark or alter those accepted answers.
