# Review and verification

Independent local review reproduced an unhandled pre-write failure in a checkpoint attempt. The corrected UI catches start, attempt and completion errors, retains the relevant session and gives operation-specific uncertainty guidance. A synchronous busy guard prevents repeated in-screen mutations; controls remain focusable during the wait and failures. Four browser tests cover pre-write failures and recovery, a simulated evidence failure after an attempt was persisted, and a simulated failed transaction notification after completion actually committed. The latter verified persisted checkpoint completion and unlocked lesson, the warning, feedback and focus, and a reload to Home. The simulation is not a claim that a real browser reports commits this way.

Independent review separately tested post-write attempt and completion failures, repeated clicks and blocked in-flight interactions. Two component tests cover repeated starts and a post-save start failure. The full local unit suite passed 421 tests with one skipped; lint passed. Focus after successful checkpoint submission remains a separate reproduced issue. Refresh, external navigation, other tabs and operations started elsewhere are not coordinated. No formal approval was granted.
