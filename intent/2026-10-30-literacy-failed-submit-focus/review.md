# Review and verification

The real browser reproduction lost focus to the body when a focused submit button became disabled and saving failed. After the change, two consecutive failed Enter submissions returned focus to the button and a third succeeded. A delayed request with deliberate interaction in empty space retained focus on the body. A component test also verifies a deliberately focused exit control remains focused. Independent review found the first implementation stole focus after an empty-space click; the corrected version cancels restoration on subsequent pointer or focus interaction. The independent reviewer reran seven related tests and the browser cases. Lint passed. Screen-reader speech and interruption across navigation were not verified; no formal approval is implied.
