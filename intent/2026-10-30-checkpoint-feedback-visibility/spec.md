# Specification

After an incorrect recheck initiated from the focused submit control, move focus to the updated feedback only if the learner has not interacted during the write. Preserve the existing focus on the next action after success, but cancel automatic focus when the learner deliberately scrolls, uses scrolling keys, moves focus or clicks while waiting. Preserve initial-failure and save-error behavior. Verify mobile/desktop recheck geometry, keyboard navigation, delayed scroll/focus, successful progression and independent review.
