# Specification

On the transient result screen, group available failed-check messages from accepted activity results by their activity, identify the original instruction and distinguish these from fully correct success messages. If an accepted result contains failed checks with no specific messages, say the detail is unavailable; do not invent an explanation. Do not show the section for fully correct results, include raw answers, change scores or persist new data. Verify a complete lesson, no-details and all-correct cases, responsiveness and independent review.
