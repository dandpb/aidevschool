A safe fix needs broader concurrency and reconciliation design and tests; it is not counted as a completed cycle.

An early fragment-link version was corrected after review showed it left a misleading URL after leaving the map. The shipped control focuses the row without changing the URL.

Independent review reproduced an early review-rescheduling issue during cycle 8; cycle 9 implements and verifies its correction.

Independent local audit also found browser Back can leave the SPA during an activity instead of navigating to its map. The existing intro discloses that unsent answers are transient; history behavior remains unresolved and was not counted as a completed cycle. A safe change requires considering in-flight writes, review/challenge sessions, result data and hosted navigation.

Independent local review also reproduced that the standalone intermediate route leaves l01 locked while the current track-completion achievement requires all ready IA lessons. Whether completing the chosen route should count, or the skipped lesson should become available, needs a product decision; statuses and the achievement rule are unchanged. A synthetic state was used, not a complete playthrough, and hosted behavior was not verified.

An independent local review also found that completing one lesson review can reschedule multiple skills using the lesson's highest starting stage, including a skill not yet due. Existing tests explicitly encode that stage selection; whether progression should instead be per skill or only for due skills needs a separate product and compatibility decision. No scheduling rule was changed here.
