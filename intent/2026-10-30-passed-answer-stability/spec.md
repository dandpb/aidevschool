# Specification

- Keep a passed answer visible but non-editable in both lesson and checkpoint players until the learner advances; explain that the approved response is ready for the next action.
- Guard same-event edits as well as rendering disabled controls; do not change scoring, evidence or navigation and do not disable changes after failed answers.
- Verify lesson and checkpoint, keyboard progression and attempts to edit; preserve existing error/retry behavior.
- Text responses remain focusable and selectable as read-only after approval, with an associated explanation; pending operations still disable editing.
