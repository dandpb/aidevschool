# Independent review

A reviewer reproduced local deep-link switching failures before and after the write. Previously both silently displayed IA; after a simulated post-commit rejection, storage already said Dev. The implementation now reads the authoritative progress after the switch settles, derives both route and displayed progress from that snapshot, warns on uncertain switches and stops with recovery if the reread fails. It does not retry the failed switch automatically.

Independent review found a stale successful snapshot on a synthetic same-instance rerender; a test reproduced 0 XP instead of 99 and success-ref cleanup corrected it. Review then identified a delayed old read that could trigger a duplicate switch after cleanup; a deterministic test reproduced two calls and the code now captures the in-flight operation before the read. The revised tests cover StrictMode cancellation, pending and settled re-entry, one write, the delayed stale read and resumed in-progress lesson. A rejected operation can still yield an old warning if the same AppShell instance is synthetically restarted; it rereads progress rather than applying old state. Production App fixes its host/services at mount, and neither separate mounts nor cross-tab coordination are claimed.

Before-fix tests failed; after the final change, 26 focused tests, two local browser cases (including simulated post-commit rejection), full suite (489 passed, one skipped), lint/build and independent review passed. Real screen-reader speech and actual storage faults were not tested; the build retains its large-chunk warning.
